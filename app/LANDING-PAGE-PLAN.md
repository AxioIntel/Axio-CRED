# Axio-CRED landing page and dashboard

## Strategy
Value proposition: Axio-CRED helps local businesses, agencies and independent researchers understand reputation changes without juggling disconnected listings and review evidence.
Goal: let visitors explore the working local demo. The skill's trial/signup conversion pattern is adapted to the actual preview; no pretend signup, email capture, or payment completion.
Objections: capability is answered by interactive product examples and explicit availability; cost by the plan table; trust by provenance and manual evidence review. No customer testimonials, logos, certifications or performance claims have been supplied, so these cannot be invented.
Differentiation: evidence inspection and assisted reporting across business profiles, including competitors without ownership. Not a claim of uniqueness against named vendors.

## Visual system and wireframe
Dark teal base, one mint accent, DM Sans, 8px spacing, 1200px max content; light pricing and closing CTA. Scoped styles preserve the existing dashboard. Keyboard focus, reduced motion, mobile menu and horizontal table scrolling are required.
15 section skill structure: announcement; navigation; hero and illustrative product preview; supported/planned source strip (replaces endorsement logos); problems/benefits; interactive feature showcase; three-step journey; factual plan metrics; evidence principles (replaces unprovided testimonials); evidence/workflow deep dives; integration availability; pricing and expandable feature comparison; FAQ; final demo CTA; footer.
No fabricated setup times, annual savings, customer counts, guarantees or compliance badges. No analytics/session recording added without configuration. Privacy/terms remain production launch work; local preview notices describe the current state without inventing legal promises.

## Approved wording
Business $49/month: one monitored business, checked twice a day for changes. Growth $149/month: five monitored businesses, checked four times a day for changes. No frequency selector. Each Google business can link Facebook and Trustpilot without another paid slot. Frequency is fixed by plan; scheduler remains pending and visibly marked. Enterprise unlimited business allowance is commercial scope with capacity and implementation pending.

## Dashboard improvements
Add real workspace usage, last collection timestamp and partial-sample coverage, platform connection access, and a plan frequency label with explicit automation state. Keep existing dataset evidence and enrichment paths intact.

## QA
Build, focused tests of navigation/comparison/frequency and dashboard unknown states; Chromium at 375, 390, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440, 1920px and 125/150 percent equivalent layouts. Keyboard/mobile menu, tabs, CTA routing, reduced motion and browser console checks. Safari/iOS/Firefox, Lighthouse and production legal/analytics remain unverified unless actually run.
